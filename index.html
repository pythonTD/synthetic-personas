About

Overview

Matias Volonte is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and a faculty member in the Biomedical Data Science and Informatics (BDSI) program at Clemson University. He directs the Synthetic Personas Lab, which studies human interaction with embodied conversational agents across extended reality (XR) platforms, including immersive and non-immersive environments. His research focuses on applications in education and digital health interventions.

His work has appeared in venues including IEEE Virtual Reality (VR), IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (TVCG), Intelligent Virtual Agents (IVA), and the ACM Symposium on Applied Perception.

Focus areas

Research Interests

Embodied & Intelligent Agents

Virtual humans and conversational agents that interpret and respond to verbal and non-verbal user behavior.

Human-AI Interaction

How people build trust in, adapt to, and are influenced by AI-driven interactive systems.

AI in Education & Health

Applied deployments of virtual agents in classrooms and digital health interventions.

Eye Tracking

Gaze and visual attention as a channel for understanding and shaping human-agent interaction.

Cross-Cultural & Multilingual Interaction

Language familiarity and cultural background as factors in agent design and user response.

Extended Reality

Virtual, mixed, and augmented reality as platforms for embodied interaction research.
