Lab overview

Synthetic Personas Lab

The Synthetic Personas Lab develops intelligent, empathetic virtual humans capable of natural, human-like interaction. Our work spans healthcare, education, and cross-cultural and multilingual communication, focused on agents that interpret verbal and non-verbal input, adapt to individual users, and provide meaningful support in real-world settings. By advancing how people interact with embodied AI, the lab aims to make human-AI interaction more intuitive, effective, and trustworthy — across both immersive and non-immersive environments.

Current directions

What we're working on

Health behavior change agents

Embodied conversational agents for patient education, chronic pain intervention, and storytelling-based health motivation.

Emotion contagion in virtual humans

How appearance fidelity and affective behavior in virtual humans shape emotional response in interpersonal simulations.

Multilingual & cross-cultural agents

Effects of language familiarity and cultural background on user response to virtual crowds and dialogue agents.

Gaze & nonverbal behavior

Eye tracking as a lens on visual attention and emotion during human–virtual human interaction.
